集字之法The Jízì Method

集字者，集古人之字也。每一件作品皆由有據可考的歷代碑帖真跡字形集結而成，絕不杜撰新字。筆畫有來歷，結體有出處。

Every work is assembled from attested historical glyphs — traced to steles, albums and rubbings — never invented. Each stroke has a pedigree; each structure has a source.

此法的先聲是唐懷仁集王羲之書《聖教序》：歷二十餘年，從右軍遺墨中一字一字選出、摹勒上石，成為千古集字之典範。

The precedent is the Tang-dynasty Preface to the Sacred Teachings (聖教序): the monk Huairen spent over two decades selecting and engraving individual characters from Wang Xizhi's surviving ink — the enduring model of glyph composition.

用印之則The One-Seal Rule

一件作品，只用一方朱印。印不奪字，紅不蓋墨；多印則俗。

One work, one red seal. The seal serves the calligraphy — vermilion must never overpower ink. More than one seal turns refinement into clutter.
