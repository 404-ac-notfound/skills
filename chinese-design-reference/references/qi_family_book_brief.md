# Qi Family Book — Application Brief (齊氏家族紀念冊 應用簡報)

**Status of this document:** every recommendation here is **(D) proposed contemporary design application** — design options derived from the research modules, *not* historical facts. Historical evidence lives in the modules and the 齊 dossier; this brief deliberately contains no new factual claims.

**Project:** Chinese–English family-history book for the Qi/Chee 齊 family, roots in Qi'an Village, Fuzhou (village-name characters to be confirmed by the family).

---

## 1. Title and cover composition (D)

- **Book title calligraphy:** commission or carefully construct a 行書/楷書 title treatment of the family name and book title. Any non-standard 齊 must follow the attested-variant workflow (Module 01 §6) and the client's documented preference (dossier §5: two lower horizontal levels, left-leg upward finish, right-leg downward finish) — labelled as a *family design choice*, not a historical form.
- **Cover layout option A (classical):** vertical title block, upper right; a single 朱文 name seal below the title; generous 留白; no decorative border. Rationale: album-leaf and thread-bound conventions (Module 06 §1).
- **Cover layout option B (bilingual modern):** horizontal Chinese title dominant, English subtitle at 55–65% of the Chinese point size, letterspaced small caps; seal as the single red accent. Hierarchy per Module 06 §2.
- **Palette discipline:** ink black, paper white/cream, seal-paste red as the *only* accent (Module 05 §6 on 印泥 qualities). Resist adding "Chinese red and gold" ornament — see the anti-cliché argument in Module 04 §6.

## 2. Bilingual page design (D)

- **Structure:** Chinese primary, English secondary (the family's Malaysian readers are bilingual but the book's subject is Chinese heritage). Options: (a) facing-page Chinese/English; (b) single-column parallel with English set smaller and in a cooler grey-black. Facing-page is safer for navigation.
- **Fonts (licences verified in Module 08 §6):** Source Han Serif TC (Traditional Chinese body) paired with a quiet Latin serif; LXGW WenKai for quotation/evocative display only, never body; Noto Sans TC for captions and family-tree labels. All OFL — cleared for publication with no fee.
- **Chinese body size:** ≥ 10.5 pt (五號) per CLREQ-derived guidance (Module 08 §2); never below 7.875 pt even for captions.
- **Punctuation & line-breaking:** full-width 。、「」,《》 with GB/T 15834-2011 forms; enforce 避頭尾 prohibitions (Module 06 §§4–5). Vertical text only for short display elements (chapter openers), never for body prose.

## 3. Photographs (D)

- Scan originals at **≥ 600 dpi** (FADGI-derived; Module 08 §4). Restoration limited to dust/scratch removal and tonal repair — no content alteration; log every intervention.
- Portrait pages: cream ground, thin keyline or none; caption in both languages; dated where known, "date unknown" where not — do not guess.
- Dark-background pages are high-risk for reversed CJK text: minimum 0.5 pt reversed strokes, rich black C60 M40 Y40 K100 only for areas ≥ 5 mm, total ink ≤ 300% (Module 08 §3).

## 4. Family tree (世系圖) (D)

- Use the horizontal generational diagram strategy for wide trees (Module 08 §5): generation bands top-to-bottom, siblings left-to-right by seniority; fold-out or spread-across-two-pages for width.
- **Names in three coexisting forms** where documented: Chinese characters, registered Malaysian spelling, pinyin — with an explicit note (per Module 07 §6) that spellings vary by dialect romanisation and registration era, and that no identity inference should be made from spelling alone.
- Do not "correct" historical spellings (e.g., a registered "Chee") to pinyin; record as-is.

## 5. Seals (D)

- **Family seal option:** a 白文 square seal 「齊氏之印」 or 朱文 「齊」 single-character mark — both are documented conventions (Module 05 §§1–2). Legibility check: 齊 in 小篆-derived seal script is dense; test at 12 mm and 18 mm before committing (Module 05 §3).
- Placement: lower-left of title calligraphy, or on the colophon page — never scattered decoratively (the Qianlong cautionary case, Module 05 §5).
- One red, used sparingly. No blue/purple paste without a documented reason (colour conventions vary; Module 05 §§6–7).

## 6. Imagery (D)

- If a landscape is wanted (e.g., evoking Fuzhou/閩江), commission or select a work that uses 平遠 composition and genuine 留白 rather than stock "misty mountains" clipart (Module 04 §§2, 4, 6). Fuzhou-specific imagery (ancestral-hall photographs, Min-river motifs) outranks generic "Chinese" imagery (Module 07).
- Reusable cleared images already in this library: see `images/` and the licence column of `assets_register.csv`.

## 7. Open items the family must supply

1. Exact characters for "Qi'an Village" (齊安村?) and the village's county.
2. Client artwork of the preferred 齊 form, for a true style-matching assessment (dossier §8 lists what is needed).
3. Registered name spellings as they appear on Malaysian documents for each family member included.
4. Any inscriptions (gravestones, ancestral-hall tablets) to be reproduced — photograph in raking light where possible.
